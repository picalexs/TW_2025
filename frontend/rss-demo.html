🔧 API Endpoints

Use these endpoints to integrate RSS feeds into your applications:

General Pet Feeds:

GET /api/rss/pets?type={recent|popular}&species={species}&zone={zone}&breed={breed}&limit={number}

Trending Pets:

GET /api/rss/trending?zone={zone}&species={species}&breed={breed}&limit={number}&period={days}

Location-based Popular:

GET /api/rss/popular-by-location?city={city}&country={country}&radius={km}&limit={number}

Breed-specific Recent:

GET /api/rss/recent-by-breed?species={species}&breed={breed}&limit={number}

Sharing Links:

GET /api/rss/share?type={type}&zone={zone}&breed={breed}&species={species}
